Membership Logic

Lumina Membership Logic

Lumina Platinum Access is a private, client-specific layer connected to the individual and the residence.

It is not publicly purchasable, publicly priced or transferable as a conventional membership.

Services are activated according to the approved profile, location, professional availability and the individual commercial terms of each arrangement.

Access

How Private Client Access Begins

Following the first official meeting, formal acceptance, execution of the required agreements and receipt of the initial payment, the client receives secured access credentials to a private La Doña Santa portal.

The first stage is a guided Development Screening completed together with the La Doña Santa team.

Once completed, the portal becomes the client’s live project environment — providing access to construction progress, weekly reports, project communication, approvals, uploaded documents, site intelligence and, where available, secured live camera access.

The client is able to communicate through the portal with the assigned project leadership, including the authorised operational and project-management team.

The Application

The La Doña Santa Application

When the residence enters operational life, the private portal changes from Development Mode into Residence Mode.

From the secured client environment, the owner receives access to download and activate the La Doña Santa application.

The La Doña Santa application becomes the continuing interface for residence management, maintenance, staff coordination, wellness scheduling, private services, lifestyle preferences, guest preparation, travel support and long-term Guardian care.

After this, the visitor enters the company itself.
