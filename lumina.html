Lumina Platinum Access

The residence is only one layer of the private world created around you.

Lumina Platinum Access extends the La Doña Santa system beyond architecture into recovery, longevity, movement, nutrition, ritual, travel, residence operations and highly personalized private care. It is not a fixed package or a conventional concierge catalogue. Every access profile is configured around the client’s biology, schedule, location, privacy, family, residence and preferred way of living.

01

Private wellness and longevity

A private wellness structure may be formed around the client through carefully selected therapies, recovery practices, movement systems, technologies and practitioners. Access may include massage, therapeutic bodywork, somatic and craniosacral sessions, breathwork, meditation, yoga, private training, mobility, recovery, hydrotherapy, thermal and cold experiences, light-based recovery, sleep support, nervous-system restoration and longevity-focused programs. Services may take place within the residence, through selected partner environments or through privately arranged international access.

02

Masters, therapists and ceremonial access

La Doña Santa may provide access to selected therapists, holistic masters, traditional healers, shamans, curanderos, spiritual teachers, gurus, ceremonial practitioners and retreat facilitators from an international private network. The objective is not entertainment or spiritual spectacle. It is access to serious practitioners whose work may support a meaningful private process.

03

In-residence practitioners

Selected practitioners may visit the residence on scheduled dates or remain within the property during an agreed period of treatment, retreat, training or recovery. The private team may include therapists, trainers, bodyworkers, movement specialists, wellness practitioners, recovery professionals, ceremonial guides and other approved specialists required by the client’s program.

04

Private culinary life

Private chefs, nutrition specialists and culinary teams may be integrated into the life of the residence. This may include daily chef service, therapeutic nutrition, performance nutrition, longevity-focused menu design, ingredient sourcing, private dining, event dining, dietary protocols, guest preparation and personal menu development. The culinary system is shaped around the client’s preferences, health direction, cultural requirements, travel schedule and use of the residence.

05

Movement, performance and body design

Movement is structured as part of the client’s living environment rather than separated from it. Private programs may include strength, mobility, body design, rehabilitation support, conditioning, yoga, breathwork, recovery integration, calisthenics, performance training and long-term physical development. Training may be delivered by resident or visiting professionals inside the client’s private gym and movement environment.

06

Private mobility and aviation

Where available, La Doña Santa may coordinate the movement surrounding the client and residence. This may include private drivers, secured transfers, helicopter charter, private aviation coordination, airport handling, yacht or marine transport, arrival management and location-specific mobility support. All arrangements remain subject to availability, operator approval, jurisdiction and separate commercial terms.

07

Private journeys and experiences

Private travel, cultural access, retreats, landscape journeys, wellness destinations, ceremonial visits and individually designed international experiences may be arranged through the client’s private access profile. The experience is structured around the client rather than sold as a public itinerary.

08

Residence operations and Guardian care

Guardian protects the operational life of the completed residence. Its role may include preventive maintenance, technical servicing, housekeeping, landscape care, pool and water-system management, staff coordination, security coordination, residence preparation, arrival readiness, guest requirements and long-term preservation of the property. The purpose is to preserve not only the physical building, but the standard, atmosphere and intelligence for which it was created.

09

Personal program architecture

No two Lumina Platinum profiles are identical. The client’s private profile determines which therapies, practitioners, rituals, travel arrangements, culinary systems, movement programs, operational services and care layers are activated. The profile may evolve as the client’s biology, schedule, residence, family and priorities change.

Membership logic

A private layer, not a public membership

Lumina Platinum Access is a private, client-specific layer connected to the individual and the residence. It is not publicly purchasable, publicly priced or transferable as a conventional membership. Services are activated according to the approved profile, location, professional availability and the individual commercial terms of each arrangement.

How private client access begins

From the first meeting to a living project

Following the first official meeting, formal acceptance, execution of the required agreements and receipt of the initial payment, the client receives secured access credentials to a private La Doña Santa portal. The first stage is a guided Development Screening completed together with the La Doña Santa team. Once completed, the portal becomes the client’s live project environment — providing construction progress, weekly reports, project communication, approvals, uploaded documents, site intelligence and, where available, secured live camera access.
