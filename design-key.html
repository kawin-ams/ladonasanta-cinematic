Client Design Key

The first private step in shaping your La Doña Santa residence.

Every La Doña Santa residence begins with the person who will live within it. This guided profiling process allows the team to understand your rhythm, privacy, relationships, sensory preferences, wellness direction, way of living and the deeper qualities the residence must support. Your responses become part of the design intelligence from which the architecture is formed.

This screening is completed once, together with an authorized La Doña Santa team member — inside the office or through an approved guided process. After completion it is locked; only an authorized administrator may reopen it.
